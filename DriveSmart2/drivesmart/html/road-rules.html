Must know

Important road rules.

1. Always wear a seat belt / helmet
Driver and all passengers must wear seat belts. Both riders on a two-wheeler must wear a helmet.
2. Follow the speed limit
Inside the city the limit is usually 50 km/h. Near schools and hospitals it is 25 km/h.
3. No mobile phone while driving
Using a phone while driving can lead to a fine of Rs. 5000. Park the car first if you need to call.
4. Keep left, overtake from right
In India we drive on the left side. Only overtake from the right side and give an indicator.
5. Never drink and drive
It is the most dangerous mistake on the road. Do not drive at all if you have been drinking.
