About DriveSmart

More than driving lessons.
A smarter way to learn.

DriveSmart started in 2018 with just two cars and one trainer. Today we have more than 15 instructors and have trained over 500 learners in Jalandhar and nearby cities.

We believe that good drivers are made, not born. That is why every learner gets a training plan made according to their own speed.

  • Experienced and certified instructors
  • Personal training plan for every learner
  • Safety first - theory classes on road rules
  • Help with learner and permanent licence

Our Mission

To make every learner a safe and responsible driver, not just someone who can pass the test.

Our Vision

To reduce road accidents by spreading road safety awareness in every family we teach.

How it works

Your learning journey.

From your first day to your driving licence, in 5 simple steps.

  1. Enroll - Fill the registration form and choose your course.
  2. Theory Class - Learn signs, signals and basic car parts.
  3. Practice - Drive on an empty ground with your instructor.
  4. Road Training - Drive in real traffic with dual control safety.
  5. Licence Test - We prepare you and help with the RTO test.
